<h1>Technology in Education</h1>

<td>Abstract</td>
<td>
Technology has changed education by making learning more interactive and accessible. Students can use computers, tablets, and online platforms to learn at their own pace. Teachers use digital tools to create engaging lessons and track progress. It also allows remote learning. However, challenges include distractions, unequal access, and overuse of screens. When used properly, technology improves learning and prepares students for the future.
</td>
</tr>

<td>Abstract</td>
<td>
Technology in education improves access to knowledge and supports personalized learning. Students can explore topics deeply and develop critical thinking skills. Teachers benefit from better tools for instruction and communication. However, there are concerns about screen time, digital inequality, and reduced face-to-face interaction. A balance between technology and traditional learning is important for effective education.
</td>
