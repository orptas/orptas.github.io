Social Media and Children

URL UNICEF
Author UNICEF
Abstract Social media has a strong influence on children. It helps them connect and learn, but it also exposes them to risks such as cyberbullying, harmful content, and reduced face-to-face interaction. Excessive use can affect sleep, focus, and emotional health. Parents and teachers should guide children in using social media safely and responsibly. When used in moderation, it can support learning and communication, but balance is very important for healthy development.

URL APA
Author American Psychological Association
Abstract Research shows social media can impact children’s mental health. It may increase anxiety, comparison with others, and lower self-esteem. It also reduces real-life interaction when overused. However, it can also support creativity and connection when used properly. Monitoring and setting limits helps children build healthy online habits and protect their mental well-being.
