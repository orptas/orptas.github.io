<h1>Screen Time and Mental Health</h1>

<table border="1">
<tr>
<td>URL</td>
<td><a href="https://www.cdc.gov">CDC</a></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Author</td>
<td>Centers for Disease Control and Prevention</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Abstract</td>
<td>
Excessive screen time can affect mental and physical health. It may lead to sleep problems, reduced physical activity, and increased anxiety or depression. Too much screen use can also impact attention and real-life relationships. Health experts recommend balancing screen time with physical activity and face-to-face communication to support overall well-being.
</td>
</tr>
</table>

<td>Mayo Clinic</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Abstract</td>
<td>
Screen time can have both positive and negative effects. While devices help with learning and entertainment, too much use can lead to stress, anxiety, and poor sleep. It can also reduce focus and social interaction. Setting limits and taking breaks helps maintain a healthy balance. A mix of online and offline activities is important for mental health.
</td>
</tr>
